Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding working models, milestones, and technical guarantees.

How do your multi-vendor Amazon/Flipkart & Meesho solutions work?

I build custom marketplace engines where sellers have their own autonomous dashboard to upload products, monitor inventory, and fulfill orders. The platform automatically calculates commission splits and payouts. For the Meesho model, independent resellers can generate tracked catalog links with their own added profit margin to share on WhatsApp and Instagram, complete with Cash-on-Delivery (COD) verification algorithms.

What guarantees do you offer regarding the 7 years of experience?

Every contract includes clean, thoroughly commented code, full Git version history, NDA confidentiality, milestone-based payments (you only release funds upon approved deliverables), and 30 to 60 days of complimentary post-launch support and bug fixes.

Can you handle both web development AND Meta Ads together?

Yes, this is my highest-leverage advantage. Most agencies separate developers from marketers, leading to tracking issues and slow landing page tweaks. As a full-stack engineer and performance marketer, I configure server-side Meta Conversions API (CAPI), optimize checkout speed to 99+ PageSpeed scores, and build dynamic funnels that maximize ad ROAS immediately.

Do you build with Shopify Liquid or Headless Next.js?

Both! Depending on your store’s budget and technical needs, I develop either natively inside Shopify 2.0 with custom Liquid sections (easier for non-technical staff to manage) or build a high-performance headless architecture with Next.js and Shopify’s Storefront GraphQL API for blazing-fast page loads.
